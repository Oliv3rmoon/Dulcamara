Dulcamara Eclipse **2.1.5** (`PPSA75483`) restores PS5 sandbox unlock for `/data/dulcamara`, eliminates the PS5 close-app kernel panics, auto-relaunch loop, `ENOSYS` backup/download errors and *Champion of Realms* `freebsd` crash reported in 2.1.x testing, extends portable libraries to extended storage, and targets the next console retest’s three reports: *Champion of Realms*’ updater stalling, its music cutting out on a menu click, and black bars in Ren’Py games.

- **PS5 close-app, force-close & overlay stability.** `plat_in_background()` now pauses game execution, drains the RADV Vulkan GPU queue (`vkDeviceWaitIdle`), drains `sceSystemServiceReceiveEvent`, and pauses SDL audio the instant the PS5 Control Center / system UI overlay opens (`system_ui_overlaid`) or the app enters background. Dulcamara now catches `SIGTERM`/`SIGHUP` from PS5 Shell force-close, exits the foreground or background loop immediately without waking a parked Ren'Py thread or blocking on scanner/cover joins, and always releases audio, controllers, and RADV Vulkan (`VK_KHR_display`) cleanly via `plat_release()` before exiting.
- **No more auto-launching the last game on relaunch.** Consumes `restart-game.txt` immediately before deferred `app_start_game()`, removes the handoff if `sceSystemServiceLoadExec` fails, and automatically clears stale handoffs older than 60 seconds so normal relaunches always open your library.
- **Automatic save backups, self-updates & itch.io Shelf downloads fixed on PS5.** Tolerates `ENOSYS` / `EINVAL` / `ENOTSUP` on `fsync()`, `statvfs()`, and `fcntl.flock()` (switching PS5 free-space checks to `_fstatfs` while keeping real I/O and out-of-space errors fail-closed), fixing both `Backup failed (Function not implemented)` and itch.io Shelf’s `Could not read or write the download` — including for already-installed itch.io Shelf folders.
- **Champion of Realms 0.113 & PC Ren’Py platform / parallel-updater compatibility.** Ren’Py 8 & 7 boot scripts now report standard Linux platform metadata (`sys.platform = "linux"`, `platform.system() == "Linux"`) on PS5 instead of `"freebsd"`, fixing `Exception: Piattaforma non supportata: freebsd`. They also cap `concurrent.futures.ThreadPoolExecutor` (`max_workers <= 4`) and throttle raw `threading.Thread` bursts with `EAGAIN`/thread-limit retry backoff to stay under the PS5’s thread ceiling (this alone did not stop the updater stalling; see the next bullet).
- **Champion of Realms’ updater no longer stalls on a stuck download.** Its in-game launcher downloads 104 archives through a small thread pool (5 workers, 4 on PS5) and relies on `urlopen(timeout=120)` raising so it can retry a stalled file. On the console, `settimeout()` asks for a non-blocking socket and is told yes while the socket stays blocking, so Python’s HTTPS read, write and handshake timeouts never fired: one connection that stopped sending held its worker for good, and once all four workers held one the update never moved again (the “Total 32” on screen was the launcher’s own progress estimate, not a limit). The Ren’Py 8 and 7 boot scripts now wrap `ssl.SSLSocket.read`/`send`/`do_handshake`: a watchdog shuts down the socket of any call that runs a second past its timeout, so it raises `socket.timeout` as on a PC and the launcher retries. Calls without a timeout, or that finish in time, are untouched. A host test runs the launcher’s download loop (104 files, 4 workers, 3 tries) against a local HTTPS server whose files 33–36 stall: the previous refresh’s scripts stop at 32/104 in that model, this build completes all 104.
- **Music no longer starved when a game starts a sound.** Starting a sound (a menu click in *Champion of Realms*) held Ren’Py’s single mixer lock while the new stream was set up (finished decode threads joined, the new one started). The app’s main loop tops up the game’s sound through the same lock, so it stalled with it and the ~70 ms of queued sound ran out. Ren’Py’s `RPS_play` now opens the stream before it takes the lock (as Ren’Py’s own `RPS_queue` does), the app’s top-up never waits for the lock (it skips that pass and asks again), Ren’Py games keep 0.15 s of sound queued instead of 0.07 s and double that (to at most 0.4 s) after each underrun, and each underrun and each hold of the lock over 20 ms is written to `dulcamara.log`. Host tests make each thread start take 300 ms (a top-up used to block 305 ms, now it never waits) and simulate underruns in both Ren’Py cores; whether thread starts are ever that slow on the console is unproven. A matched console retest is still needed to establish that the music now plays through the clicks; the new log lines say whether the lock is ever held long.
- **Black bars: two real causes of letterboxing removed; the cause of the transient flashes is not yet proven.** With **Integer scale** on, HD Ren’Py pictures were fitted by halved “lines” (a 768-line game was fitted as 192 lines, leaving bars on a 4K screen); integer scale now applies to pictures under 480 lines only, as the smoothing cut-off does. A window size saved by the game or by a PC save, together with its “maximized” flag, made Ren’Py draw the picture at a different shape and pad it with black; the console now always ignores the saved size and the maximized state in both Ren’Py cores. The PS5 OpenGL driver patch also invalidates a small software blit’s render-target destination before the CPU writes it (a gap of the same kind as the earlier texture flashes; no Ren’Py path was found that reaches it). The earlier texture-cache patch was already in the shipped core: its transfer and resource code is identical to the patched driver’s. Transient black flashes seen below 60 fps were **not** reproduced and are **not** shown to be fixed. If they persist, send `dulcamara.log` from a session with the bars: the new `screen:` line (picture, canvas, rectangle, integer scale, filtering) and any `renpy: black flash` lines say whether the black is in the game’s own picture or in the display path.
- **PS5 storage & sandbox unlock restored (`PPSA75483`).** Restores single-threaded startup unlock (`ps5_unlock` for etaHEN / LegacyJB / OnionHEN plus the `PPSA75483`-targeted `lapy.elf` helper) so `/data/dulcamara/games`, automatic save backups, and library scanning work again under ShadowMountPlus instead of failing with `Operation not permitted`. **Settings → About** now displays live storage and unlock status.
- **Triple-buffered zero-stall Vulkan rendering & crisp HD scaling for Ren’Py.** Upgrades `V.soft_image[3]` to a triple-buffered ring with swapchain `image_in_flight` fence tracking and explicit `HOST_WRITE → TRANSFER_READ` staging barriers so every fresh Ren’Py frame uploads **once** into a guaranteed-idle slot and repeated 60 Hz refreshes sample read-only with zero copy, zero stalls, and no CPU-to-GPU cache race. HD frames (`>= 480p`, Ren’Py visual novels) now use bilinear filtering (`VK_FILTER_LINEAR`) instead of RPG Maker’s nearest-neighbor pixel-art mode so UI text and portraits stay crisp on 1080p/4K TVs without dropped pixel columns (while RPG Maker 240p keeps sharp nearest-neighbor pixels by default).
- **Instant settings persistence & fixed updater.** Toggling **Test builds** (or any setting) writes `settings.ini` immediately instead of waiting for Circle to leave Settings, so closing from the PS5 home menu no longer forgets the toggle. When storage is unavailable, startup recovery now defers cleanly instead of permanently locking **Check for updates now** or showing a misleading save-recovery message on **Install itch.io Shelf**, and **Square** retry refreshes the detected app installation directory.
- **Reset-safe portable libraries on USB & extended storage.** `dulcamara/portable.txt` is now probed on extended storage (`/mnt/ext0`, `/mnt/ext1`) as well as `/usb0..7` and `/mnt/usb0..7`, with duplicate mount aliases deduplicated automatically so your games and saves can live safely off internal `/data`.
- **Includes all 2.1.x fixes and improvements.** Dedicated title ID `PPSA75483` (coexists beside vkQuake’s `PPSA99010`), *Fantasy Opposite* detection, itch.io Shelf 1.1 with PS5 console DNS resolution, *Summertime Saga* 0.20.16 & 21.0.0-wip.8194, *My New Family* 0.14 → Epilogue handoff, *Champion of Realms* 0.113, R3 **Privacy shortcut**, **Left/Right one-handed controls**, embedded Inter fallback font, and the `OPTIONS` Settings glyph.

**Installing or updating to refreshed 2.1.5.** Extract `PPSA75483.zip` and copy the `PPSA75483` folder over your existing `PPSA75483` installation (or into `/data/homebrew/PPSA75483`). Once 2.1.5 opens, open **Settings → About** to confirm **2.1.5**, **PPSA75483**, and your active storage/unlock status.

**Host verification & console testing notes.** Host suites verified PS5 `ENOSYS` `fsync`/`statvfs`/`flock` compatibility with `EIO` fail-closed controls, `restart-game.txt` single-consumption & staleness cleanup, Ren’Py `freebsd` → `linux` platform normalization, immediate `Test builds` persistence, deferred sandbox recovery, Lapy elevation wire/client tests, 2.1.4 → 2.1.5 transactional self-update using the packaged `PPSA75483.zip`, weak-import and PS5 module-import audits (`eboot.bin` + `lapy.elf` manifest SHA-256), and full GPL source archive restoration.
