A test build for Ren'Py on the PS5, after your runs of 0.2.10 (four hours without a crash). 0.2.12 is aimed at
music that played once and didn't loop, and at finding out what the black flashes and the missing characters have to
do with the frame rate and memory.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). Put your games in `/data/dulcamara/games/`, one folder each (a Ren'Py
game's whole folder). No games come with it. With **Settings > System > Test builds** on, the app offers this update
itself.

- **Music that stops looping:** on the build machine every game's music loops, so on the console something fails as
  Ren'Py starts it again, and Ren'Py then quietly stops looping it. The log now says what failed. If a game's music
  plays once, send the log and say which game.
- **Black bars and the frame rate:** you saw them whenever Show FPS fell below 60. Show FPS counts Dulcamara's own
  frames, and with a big game Dulcamara redid its copy of the game's picture every frame, even when it had not
  changed. Now it does that only for a new picture, which should keep it at 60 more often. The log notes each late
  frame and what took the time.
- **Black flashes and the frame rate:** you saw them below 60 frames a second. Big (1920x1080) games run at 16 to 22
  here, and reading each picture back takes most of that. To try a faster, smaller picture, put this line in
  `/data/dulcamara/renpy-test.txt` and start the game:
  - `DULCAMARA_PICTURE_HEIGHT=720`

  Then say whether the flashes still come. The log also notes each black flash it sees, with how long its picture
  took.
- **Characters disappearing after hours:** the log now notes each time the graphics memory runs out for an image. When
  characters go missing, send the log before starting the app again.
- **Quiet at half volume:** Ren'Py games make half of their volume sliders very quiet (a tenth of full) on every
  platform; Dulcamara plays them at their own level. Turn the game's slider up, or Dulcamara's own Volume.
- 0.2.11's `renpy-test.txt` lines still work (`DULCAMARA_GL_CPU_LOAD=0`, `DULCAMARA_MIPMAPS=0`,
  `DULCAMARA_GL_WRITE_BACK=0`, `DULCAMARA_GL_READ_DIRECT=0`), one at a time.
- Not yet tried on a console. Everything 0.2.11 has stays.

**Testers:** ask for help and say what breaks on the [nightly Discord](https://discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9). Please send
`/data/dulcamara/logs/dulcamara.log` (and `dulcamara.old.log` and `renpy-errors.txt` beside it), the game and its
version, and what you saw.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
