A test build for your two update requests: the update that kept saying "The download failed", and an update popup
like the other apps have.

**Install this one by hand:** the download fails in the updater of 0.2.12 and before, so 0.2.13 can't fix it until it
is installed. Unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). From 0.2.13 on, with **Settings > System > Test builds** on, the app
offers each new build itself. Your games, saves and settings are kept.

- **"The download failed":** the updater gave every download 25 seconds in all, and the update is 75.8 MB, so any
  connection slower than about 3 MB/s failed every time. Now it takes as long as it needs, and gives up only if
  nothing arrives for 30 seconds. If it still fails, it says why and you can try again straight away.
- **An update popup:** when Dulcamara opens and finds a newer build, it asks: **Update now** or **Later**, with the
  version and the download size. While it downloads you see how far it has got (Circle hides it; the download goes
  on, and Settings > System shows it too). When it is done, **Close Dulcamara** closes the app; open it again to use
  the new version.
- Not yet tried on a console. Everything 0.2.12 has stays: its `renpy-test.txt` lines and its log lines for the black
  flashes, music that stops looping and characters disappearing.

**Testers:** ask for help and say what breaks on the [nightly Discord](https://discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9). Please send
`/data/dulcamara/logs/dulcamara.log` (and `dulcamara.old.log` and `renpy-errors.txt` beside it), the game and its
version, and what you saw. For the update, the lines that start with `update:` and `net:` say what happened.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
