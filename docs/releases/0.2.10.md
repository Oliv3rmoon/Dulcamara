A test build for Ren'Py on the PS5. 0.2.9 fixed the crash on a game's first OGG sound, and its menu is no longer
transparent. 0.2.10 is aimed at what was left: black flashes at the borders of a game's boxes, its animations
stopping, and the app hanging as the cursor moved fast over a title menu. It also adds two things testers asked for.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). Put your games in `/data/dulcamara/games/`, one folder each (a Ren'Py
game's whole folder). No games come with it.

- Should stop the black boxes around images that should be partly clear (a cup on a table, a strip beside a name
  tag, a box's border): the console lost such an image's clear parts as Ren'Py made it ready, and Dulcamara now makes
  it ready itself.
- Should stop the black flashes at a box's animated border: a movie's picture now waits for its mask (what makes
  parts of it clear) instead of being drawn without it, black where it should be clear.
- Movies decode with fewer threads, and a movie or sound the console gives no thread for is no longer waited for:
  moving the cursor fast over a title menu should no longer freeze the app or lose its background. If it still
  does, the log now says which movie, sound or thread failed.
- **Sounds for this game:** in a Ren'Py game, Dulcamara's menu (hold Options) lists the game's sounds; Cross turns
  one off (its hover sound, say) or on again. The game's archive is not changed. Also in Settings > Ren'Py >
  Sounds for a game.
- **Settings > System > Test builds:** turn it on and the update offers test builds like this one, so you need not
  install each by hand.
- Not yet tried on a console. Everything 0.2.9 has stays.

**Testers:** ask for help and say what breaks on the [nightly Discord](https://discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9). Play a Ren'Py
game, its title screen and in game, then please send `/data/dulcamara/logs/dulcamara.log` (and `dulcamara.old.log`
beside it), and say which game and which version of it (PC, Mac or Linux build).

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
