A test build for Ren'Py on the PS5. 0.2.4 ran Ren'Py games without crashing, and their music played, but the
picture stayed black. The OpenGL library the Ren'Py cores draw with asks for the console's video output before it
draws anything, and the app already has it for its own screen; 0.2.5 gives that library a stand-in output of its
own, so it draws while the app keeps the screen. This is the likely cause, not yet a proven one, so the log now says
much more about the picture.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). Put your games in `/data/dulcamara/games/`, one folder each (a Ren'Py
game's whole folder). No games come with it.

- Aimed at 0.2.4's black picture in Ren'Py games.
- `dulcamara.log` says how much of the first pictures is not black, any OpenGL error, and what the OpenGL library
  and Python report.
- The first black seconds of a Ren'Py game are most likely it loading behind its presplash, which Dulcamara does
  not show yet.

**Testers:** ask for help and say what breaks on the [nightly Discord](https://discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9). After starting
a Ren'Py game and waiting until its menu would show (its music plays), please send
`/data/dulcamara/logs/dulcamara.log` (and `dulcamara.old.log` beside it), whether it shows or not, and say which game
and which version of it (PC, Mac or Linux build). A Ren'Py 7 or 6 game is welcome as well.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
