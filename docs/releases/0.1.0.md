The first Dulcamara, a Nightshade app: RPG Maker 2000 and 2003 games on the PS5, on EasyRPG Player, and Ren'Py 8
visual novels, on Ren'Py itself, in Nightshade's black and red.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). Put your games in `/data/dulcamara/games/`, one folder (or
`.zip`) each, and the RTP, if a game needs it, in `/data/dulcamara/rtp/2000` or `rtp/2003`. No games come
with it.

- The library finds RPG Maker 2000 / 2003 games by their `RPG_RT.ldb` and `RPG_RT.lmt`, with their titles and
  title screens, and Ren'Py 8 games by their `game/` folder.
- Ren'Py: Cross on, Circle the game's menu, L1 / R1 back and forward, R2 fast-forwards and skips read text, the
  touchpad is the mouse; saves in `/data/dulcamara/saves/renpy`.
- Cross confirms, Circle cancels, every button can be mapped; R2 fast-forwards; the touchpad opens the menu.
- The CRT and LCD shaders of Mandrake (formerly Nightshade Station), and sharp or smooth scaling.
- Updates itself from this repository's releases.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
