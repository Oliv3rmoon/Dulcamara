The first Nightshade Player: RPG Maker 2000 and 2003 games on the PS5, on EasyRPG Player, in Nightshade's
black and red.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). Put your games in `/data/nightshade-player/games/`, one folder (or
`.zip`) each, and the RTP, if a game needs it, in `/data/nightshade-player/rtp/2000` or `rtp/2003`. No games come
with it.

- The library finds RPG Maker 2000 / 2003 games by their `RPG_RT.ldb` and `RPG_RT.lmt`, with their titles and
  title screens.
- Cross confirms, Circle cancels, every button can be mapped; R2 fast-forwards; the touchpad opens the menu.
- Nightshade Station's CRT and LCD shaders, and sharp or smooth scaling.
- Updates itself from this repository's releases.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Nightshade-Player`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
