A test build for Ren'Py on the PS5, with what its first tester asked for. Since 0.2.5 Ren'Py games show their
picture and play their sound; 0.2.6 makes the controller work in them, adds a cursor and a game's own keys, gives
Ren'Py games their own place in the library, and goes after 0.2.5's crashes.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). Put your games in `/data/dulcamara/games/`, one folder each (a Ren'Py
game's whole folder). No games come with it.

**In a Ren'Py game:**

- The D-pad (or the left stick) moves between choices, Cross picks and moves the story on, Circle or Options opens
  the game's menu and goes back out of it, L1 and R1 roll back and forward, Square hides the text window, Triangle
  turns skipping on and off, and R2 fast-forwards.
- L3 turns the cursor on and off: the left stick moves a pointer and Cross clicks where it points. The touchpad
  moves it too.
- A game's own keys (its inventory's, say) can be put on the controller's buttons for that game: its menu (the
  touchpad) > Keys for this game.

**In the library:** Ren'Py games have their own section beside the RPG Maker games; Up and Down switch between them.

**Fixes and changes:**

- Fast-forward in a Ren'Py game is Ren'Py's own skip, no longer the game run up to 8 times a frame: turning it on
  and off crashed 0.2.5 and then the console. Until a log shows that this is gone, please be careful with it.
- Ren'Py 8 games make no mipmaps unless they ask for them: on the PS5 they were made on the CPU, slowly, and 0.2.5's
  silent crashes came right after.
- `dulcamara.log` now keeps what the OpenGL library says before it ends the app, Ren'Py's own log (how long each
  stage of a game's start took), and the way each button press goes into Ren'Py.

**Testers:** ask for help and say what breaks on the [nightly Discord](https://discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9). After playing
a Ren'Py game a little (the menu with the D-pad and Cross, the cursor with L3, fast-forward with R2), please send
`/data/dulcamara/logs/dulcamara.log` (and `dulcamara.old.log` beside it), whether it works or not, and say which
game and which version of it (PC, Mac or Linux build). If a launch ends on its own, send the logs from then too.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
