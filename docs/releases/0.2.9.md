A test build for Ren'Py on the PS5. 0.2.8's log showed where a game ends as it plays its first OGG sound (a button's
hover sound, say): in FFmpeg's Vorbis decoding, at the sound's first packet, and not in reading the game's archive
(the game's MP3 music and WebM movie came from archives too and played). Every Vorbis packet goes through FFmpeg's FFT,
which looked up a table kept among its own code, and the console does not let an app read its own code. 0.2.9 keeps
that table with FFmpeg's other data.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA99010.zip` and copy its `PPSA99010` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`
(or let Nightshade's Settings > Apps do it). Put your games in `/data/dulcamara/games/`, one folder each (a Ren'Py
game's whole folder). No games come with it.

- Plays a Ren'Py game's OGG sounds (Vorbis, and Opus too): the crash 0.2.6 to 0.2.8 had as a game played its first
  one. Not yet tried on a console; the build machine plays OGG sounds through the same code. Please put back an archive you deleted.
- A movie gets 4 threads to decode with instead of 16: in 0.2.8's log the console refused the threads for the menu's
  movie when it was opened a second time, and its picture could not be decoded. If the flashing or black frames are
  still there, say where.
- Everything 0.2.8 has stays: the presplash while a game loads, the on-screen keyboard for a name, a game's
  Fullscreen doing nothing, the pause while another app has the screen, the memory line every 3 minutes, and the
  traces of what ends a game.

**Testers:** ask for help and say what breaks on the [nightly Discord](https://discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9). Start a Ren'Py
game, move on its menu and play on a little, then please send `/data/dulcamara/logs/dulcamara.log` (and
`dulcamara.old.log` beside it), and say which game and which version of it (PC, Mac or Linux build).

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA99010.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara`, or against
`SHA256SUMS`.
