Dulcamara, a Nightshade app - RPG Maker 2000 / 2003 games on the PS5
Copyright (C) 2026 Oliv3rmoon, and the authors of what it is built from. GNU General Public License v3 or later
(LICENSE-Dulcamara.txt).
Source code: https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara (the release's tag holds the exact source, with
every component below as a submodule, in third_party/, or pinned by its build script).

Dulcamara is built on Mandrake (formerly Nightshade Station, https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Mandrake), which is
darkxex's SwanStationPS5 (https://github.com/darkxex/SwanStationPS5, GPL-3.0-or-later) on BlackBearReloaded's
ps5-native-app-boilerplate (GPL-3.0-or-later): its platform layer, Vulkan screen, shaders and update check.
